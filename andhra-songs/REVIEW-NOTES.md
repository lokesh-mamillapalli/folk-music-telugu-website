# Translation review notes

All 102 songs have line-by-line English translations and English and Telugu summaries in `content/NNN.json`. Every file passes `validate_content.py`.

The lines below use rare dialect words, possibly mis-transcribed lyrics, or unclear place and person names. The translators gave their best guess for each. A Telugu speaker who knows the dialect should check them. To fix a line, edit it on the website (Admin → song page → Admin Quick Edit) or in `content/NNN.json` before importing.

Line numbers refer to `lyrics/NNN.txt`.

## Titles to be aware of
- **030 Namate Vinikove:** the lyrics sing "నా మాట ఇనిరావే". The title is spelled "నా మాటే వినికోవే" to match the English name.
- **051 Nandagiri Bangarumma:** the lyrics sing "నందగిరి బంగారు మామ", so that is the Telugu title. The English title may be a mis-romanisation.
- **065, 066, 067, 069, 087, 090, 091, 102:** the Telugu title follows the lyric wording, e.g. 066 "డీలు డీలు" for "Dellu Dellu", 087 lyrics say "నీ అత్తారింటికెళ్ళి".
- **082 Ambaa Palukulu:** these words never appear in the lyrics. The song is a wake-up song for Krishna.
