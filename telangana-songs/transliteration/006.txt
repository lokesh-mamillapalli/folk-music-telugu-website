Pallavi (Chorus):
O penlama naa penlama Mandi kantle mannu voyyagadane O penlama naa penlama O penlama naa penlama Manala chusi oorva paayiri gadane O penlama naa penlama O penlama naa penlama Marugu mandu petti malipiri gadane O penlama naa muddu penlama O penlama naa muddu penlama
Charanam 1:
Pendli gaaka mundu iddaramu kalisi sengo pilla antu sekkarlu kottanga Sengo pilla antu sekkarlu kottanga Kandla pettukoni kattagattukoni chichupetti pendli chedagotta chusiri Chichupetti pendli chedagotta chusiri Laggamayyi manamu kalisi unte oorvaka baggu baggumani mandiri gadane O penlama naa penlama bavurupoleshalu vesiri gadane O penlama naa penlama
Charanam 2:
Ye raakshasi nannu thelavaka marigindo nee meeda manasu lekunta jese Nee meeda manasu lekunta jese Chedagottu manushulu neeku mandu petti naa meeda moju lekunta jesiri Naa meeda moju lekunta jesiri Pachanga manamu koodi unte jusi kachagatti chichu lepindru gadane O penlama naa penlama idupu kaagitham daaka thechindru gadane O penlama naa penlama
Charanam 3:
Pendlayina kotthala aadivaaramotthe addha sheru saara dechukoni manamu Addha sheru saara dechukoni manamu Thalupulesukoni nadiyintla koosoni iddaramu kalisi poddantha thaaguthumu Iddaramu kalisi poddantha thaaguthumu Rendu kilalo kodi daggarikandi rendu pootala kammanga thindume O penlama naa penlama thellandaaka muchatlaadudumu kaade O penlama naa penlama
Charanam 4:
Kooli pani cheyya chethagaani nenu koosundi thinna yemana poduvu Koosundi thinna yemana poduvu Naaliki gunji kallu thaaga neeku chillaradugangane chethula peduduvu Chillaradugangane chethula peduduvu Patthalaatalalla paisalu voyyi motthukunte o daarthuvu gaade O penlama naa penlama paanamole nannu chusukunnaave O penlama naa muddu penlama
Charanam 5:
Chee chee ninnu thittina naa noru padiponu ninnu kottina naa chethulu padiponu Ninnu kottina naa chethulu padiponu Aallilla maatalu pattukoni ninnu arigosa petti uripetta choosti Arigosa petti uripetta choosti Paapapu naayee chethulathoni nee ipu nindaa vaathale pedithi O penlama naa penlama thappadagi nee thalakaya valagodithi O penlama naa muddu penlama
Charanam 6:
Aligi guligi avvagaarintla nuvvunte aduka thinetoni lekka naa bathukaaye Aduka thinetoni lekka naa bathukaaye Nannidisi nuvvidiki vachina kaanunchi dikkumokku leni kukka gosaaye Dikkumokku leni kukka gosaaye Iga nenu daaga, iga nenu thiruga, iga nannu manninchi intiki raave O penlama naa penlama iguranga iddaram kalisi undame O penlama naa penlama Nalugutla padakunda koodi undame O penlama naa penlama Iguranga iddaram kalisi undame O penlama naa penlama
