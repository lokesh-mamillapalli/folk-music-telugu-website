Charanam 1 (Verse 1): Pacchani chelallo... paareti vaagullo Muripaala molakanai aadukunnaanu Goru vankala paata... koyilammala kootha Naa menu pulakincha paadindi lona Nee matti parimalam... nee challani deevena Nenenni janmalaku maruvalenu palle thalli (O palle naa thalli kalpavalli...)
Charanam 2 (Verse 2): Muthyala muggule... maa inta arugule Ammamma cheppina aa kammani kathale Vepa chettu needa... racchabanda kaada Oori manushula mamathele maruvalenu Nee anuraagame... naa aayuvu pothale Nirantharam ninne thaluchukuntaanu palle thalli (O palle naa thalli kalpavalli...)
Charanam 3 (Verse 3): Ekkadunna gaani... ae desham vellinaa Nee pilupu vinapadaga parugetthukosthaanu Nee jnaapakaalu naa gundello padilam Nee prema baatalo saaguthaanu nithyam Nuvvu leni bathukamma... goodu leni pakshamma Naa sarvasvam neeve o palle thalli
