Pallavi (Chorus): Entha cheppina theeru raithanna vyadhalu... Enni chesina theeru raithanna vyadhalu... Gundellona daachukunna gosalu theeravu... Kallallona nindina kanneellu aagavu... (Entha cheppina theeru raithanna vyadhalu...)
Charanam 1 (Verse 1): Vaana raadu varadha raadu... vaanamma karuninchadu Chemata chukkalu chindinchinaa... chenu pandadu Appula baadhalu minnante... avamaanaalu ventaade Kaadi mose eddulamme... kaalamoccheraa raithanna (Entha cheppina theeru raithanna vyadhalu...)
Charanam 2 (Verse 2): Pagalu reyi kashtapadi... mattilona maanikyam vethikithe Dalaarula paalayye... nee kashtamanthaa raithanna Gittubaatu dhara leka... gilagilalaade nee bathuku Aakali kekala naduma... allaade nee samsaaram (Entha cheppina theeru raithanna vyadhalu...)
Charanam 3 (Verse 3): Sarkaaru haameelu... kaagithalake parimitham Nee godu vine naathude... ledaaye ee lokaana Mattini nammukunnodu... mattilone kalisipoye Ninnu nammukunnowlla... gathi emouno raithanna
(Entha cheppina theeru raithanna vyadhalu... enni chesina theeru raithanna vyadhalu...)
