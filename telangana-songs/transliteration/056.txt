Charanam 2 (Verse 2): Medalona paamulanu vesinaavayya Chethilo thrishoolamu pattinaavayya Medalona paamulanu vesinaavayya Chethilo thrishoolamu pattinaavayya Bhaktula moravini raarayya naa shivayya Varamuliyya raarayya o shivayya (Ravva ravvalayya rangu ravvalayya...)
