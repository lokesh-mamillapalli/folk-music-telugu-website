Pallavi (Chorus): Aadavilona tirigeti o koyilamma.. o koyilamma.. Ma anna garu kalisinaara o koyilamma Ningilona veligeti o jabilamma.. o jabilamma.. Ma anna jaada chepparamma o jabilamma
Charanam 1: Anna leka illu allakallolamaaye Ayyaku kallu poye.. avvaku kaalu vaaye Sellememo chinnadaaye.. thelisi theliyani daaye Emi sethu endubodu o koyilamma.. o jabilamma.. Ma badhalu evaru chuthuramma o koyilamma
Charanam 2: Munachukka odavakamunde anna paniki poye Chukkalu podichinanka anna intiki vache Poddhunaga ma panaka kaya kashtam chese Atuvanti ma anna o koyilamma.. o jabilamma.. Ma kandarani chetlekké o koyilamma
Charanam 3: Karu mabbu kammi chimma cheekatayye Etu podamanna daari dikku thochaka paaye Aa cheekatlona anna senu selaka daate Ye dikku chusina gani o koyilamma.. o jabilamma.. Na anna roopu kaanaraade o koyilamma
