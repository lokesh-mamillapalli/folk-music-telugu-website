Pallavi (Chorus):
Illidisi yellipottunna.. thalli ninnidisi nenu vothunna palle Innallu nee olla perigina.. senu selakalanta saana thirigina Kanneeru pettake thalli.. kannavoorike thirigostham malli
Charanam 1 (Verse 1):
Sallani nee olla thaladachukunnanu.. edapaina nidurinchi kalalenno ganna Pakshula paatalatho paravashinchinaanu.. muddhocche ninu chusi murisipoyinaanu Paatha noothilona palle.. eetha kottinaadhi kundi thalli
Charanam 2 (Verse 2):
Vanakaalam mabbu musugu nuvvu veyanga.. musuruletthaga palle musimusi navvanga Pesara gaayalu kasaru jaamakaayalu thini.. pasidi nemali thalli theepi koothalu vini Pulakinchi poyindi thanuvu.. aalakinchindi haayiga manasu
Charanam 3 (Verse 3):
Nee matti kougita mamakaramu panchi.. maruvaleni madhuranubhoothinicchavu E desham egina ekkada nenunna.. nanuganna naa palle ninu maruvabonamma Thalli ninnu vadhili velthunte.. gunde ninduga dukhame ninde
