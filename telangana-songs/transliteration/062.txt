Pallavi:
Muddula bavayyo na chandamamayya
Vaddaku ravayyo na chinnari mamayya
Nannu vadili petti ontariga nuvvu ekkadunnavo
E cheekati palleku deepamayyi veluguthunnavo
Nee prema emayera na chandamamayya
Nannetla marichavura na bangaru kannayya
Charanam 1:
Nenu thinakunna nannu bujjaginchi thinipinchavura
Mana pedha badhalu gurthocchi kantaneeru pettivira
Kanneellu thudichavura nenuunnanani cheppavura
Nee prema emaye avi emaipoyenura
Charanam 2:
Ee Telangana badhalu chusi ralekapoyava
Jillalloni chellellantha vaddani neetho cheppara
Poddhunne poyavura neevu rathriki vasthanani
Ekkadunnavura bava eppudu vasthavura
Charanam 3:
Mee raaka kosam eduruchudani thobbuttu ledura
Nuvvu leka iruguporugu vallu matalantu undera
Matalocchi guda ra nenu moogadanaithinira
Napai premalundi guda ra manasu champukontinira
Charanam 4:
Nee meeda na prema chavané ledamma
Podicheti aa poddhulo velugai nenu vasthanamma
Ninnu vadhili okka gadiyaina nenu undalenamma
Aa pedhala badhalu theerchi nee vodiki vasthanoyamma
