Pallavi:
Na aratukulanti leta arachhetulathoti
Arakalu pattidunnanu aa alu marichipoynanu
Charanam 1:
Badiki pothanani snanam jesi battalesukunte
Paniki pokunte poota gadavadani naya kottinde
Palakanu pagalagottinade balapam nalipivesinade
Nakunna chinni hrudayam mukkalu mukkalu chesinde
Charanam 2:
Errani endalo eruvu chimmaga kandlu thirigi padithe
Kanikaramanna leni patelu chempa meeda kotte
Naa pala buggalallo nethuru perukunnadayyo
Naa jali kandla venta kanneeru karuthunnadayyo
Charanam 3:
Sutimetthani chetha sutthe pattukoni bandalu koduthunte
Ati sunnithamaina chinni gundelo oopiragipoye
Chethulu kandipoyinayi kalina kattelayinayi
Vathalu pettinattulaye katthetho gosinattulaye
Charanam 4:
Nathoti pillalu allari chesthu adipaduthunte
Naa kanti papalu kadayi mantalo kamilipothunte
Badhipai asha chavalede inka moju theeralede
Gundela dhukkhamagadaye andala bathuku agamayye
Charanam 5:
Naa kannavallu unnollu ayithe chaduvukundunemo
Meeru pedha intlo puttadame chesina thappemo
Naa mundu mullubata venakaku chusthe murikigunta
Chaduvukunte saripodhate chadhuvunukunte vosthadhate
