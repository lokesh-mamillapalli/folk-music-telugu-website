Pallavi:
Yemi Dubai yemi Muscat-u ra naayi koduka ori Mallesha
Yentaku nuvvu gana raavemira naayi bidda na Mallesha
Charanam 1:
Illu idisi neevu poyi nallu yella daati paaye
Uttaramo yyaka pothivi phonu guda jeyya ka pothivi
Nee paanam etla unnado nuvvu akkada emi thinnavo
Yenta kashtam neeku unnado yedi maaku thelavaka paaye
Nee meeda benga paddadi ro nee thalli picchidayyindi koduka
Nityamu ninne kalavaristundi ro naayi bidda ori Mallesha
Charanam 2:
Selle pelli jesi pothivi silli gavva pampaka pothivi
Alludemo katnamu demmani sellellanu ella gottindu bidda
Polamulona laagodi ki appu thechi setu daggara
Panta pandaka thantalaaye mitthilemo perugabattere
Vaddiki vaddi chakkra vaddi ayindi ro naayi koduka ori Mallesha
Goddu godha illu polam ammina naayi bidda baaki muttadu ra
Charanam 3:
Nuvvu vanga chusuku poyina appu asalu laksha koduka
Netiki adi rendu intalaayi konda meeda koosundi bidda
Nuvvu desham poka mundu evvariki cheyi japa ka
Rajulole bathikinamu ra buju patti bathuku ayindi nedu
Etu chusina yama gandame undiro yemi sethu ori Mallesha
Bathuku meeda bhayame puduthundi ro naayi bidda na Mallesha
