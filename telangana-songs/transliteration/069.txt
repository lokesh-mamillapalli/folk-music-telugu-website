Pallavi: Pancha bhoothalu chupani bhedam manalo enduku kulamathavaadam Antha maye eppuduro idi mande gunde chappuduro
Charanam 1: Andari daggara unde nippu antaranivallu antada cheppu Aa maata ganukante nippu pondunayi lokam meppu Kaliyugamantha veluginicche surya chandrulanu chudu Kannula kondarike thana velugunu panchi inkonthariki cheekati icchuna Chudavayo tharala goppathanam manalo unnada vaatiki unna gunam
Charanam 2: Kulam matham ante prakruthe mounam manava manugada gathi anta Muttani neeru ante srushti leni edari kadha Manchithaname pakshulaku aasthi kulamu mathamani padava kusthee Buddhi nerchina manushula kanna gaddi mese gomatha minna Thana raktham paluga maarchi kammani amma premanu chupunayo
Charanam 3: Meghala premanuragale ningi nundi kuriseti chinukulu Chematorchi pani chesthe chalu nela thalli manaki-icchunu sirulu Devudu saitham kulamatha bhedam duruddheshamani korukuntada Korukunte aa korika unte piluvabadathada koluvabadathada Devunike leni ee buddhi manushullo enduku durbuddhi
Charanam 4: Palaparthi ponthapodalu adigadhigo aa konda konalu Vuniki kaniki teku chetlu annadammulamu memannatlu Guttalu jantalu pattukoni unte tegiponidi ma bandhamantavi Adavi vanamunu chudara bhai kalisi entha baguntadi royi Manushullo aikyatha ledaye kulamane kusthee manadhoka vyadhi aye
Charanam 5: Ninnu kosina nannu kosina paalu kadu velledi nethure Ninnu mosindi thommidi nelale nannu mosindi thommidi nelale Kane mundu ye thallikaina puriti noppula badha thappada Neekantene goppanollaku bhumi paina mari chavu thappada Manamantha eppudu samaname maarale maanava samajamu
