Pallavi:
Ratanala koduka raara na bangaru thandri raara
Illidisi poyina koduka nuvvu inti dari batti raara
Nuvu leka nee thalli thalladilluthundi nee thallini chusi pora
Charanam 1:
Puriti noppulu anubhavinchi allari mudduga ninu chusukunte
Purudu posi potthi guddala ninu dachi pavuranga penchukunte
Muggu buvva petti muddu maatalu cheppi uyyalalo oopi jolapata paadi
Kanti reppala kapadukunna nee thallini chuda raara
Charanam 2:
Valavala valaposthu koduka nuvvu valaselli pothivi koduka
Mana oorlo karuvu osthe koduka patnamlo pani chusukuntivi koduka
Patnamlo nee bathuku bharamayyinda.. pasthulatho nee bathuku tellaruthunda
Nuvvu leka mana illu chinnaboyindira nee avva setha puvvu vadhili pora
Charanam 3:
Ye dharini osthavani koduka nenu thovalanni chustha koduka
Eppudosthavani nee raaka koraku eduru chusthuntara koduka
Paata padukuntu polimera nundi cycle-u meeda ekki vasthunnavo
Janda gajjekada auto dhigi sopathigallatho koodi vasthunnavo
Charanam 4:
Goodidisi poyina nundi na gunde cheruvai poyindi koduka
Gundevuru ayindi koduka mana illu vakili aligindi chudaka
Illidisi poyina chinna na koduka musimusi navvula muddula koduka
Palle thalli nindu deevenalu ponda pallekokkasari vachanna pora
